★Family Point Bank

Delete your Family Point Bank account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Family Point Bank and go to Settings.
  2. At the bottom of your family's card, tap Delete my account.
  3. Type your password and tap Delete forever.

What gets deleted

If you're the only grown-up in your family: your account (email and password) and the whole family: kids, points history, rewards, calendar, settings, the invite code and the old iPad board. This happens right away and can't be undone.

If other grown-ups are in your family: your account is deleted and you're removed from the family. The family's kids, points and calendar stay for the other grown-ups, because they belong to them too.

The copy saved on your phone is removed when you delete the account or delete the app.

Can't open the app?

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it, and the data described above, within 30 days.
