★Family Point Bank

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Family Point Bank is an app for parents and other grown-ups to track points, rewards and calendars for the kids in their family. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to delete it. In short: we keep only what you type into the app, we use it only to run the app for your family, and we never sell it, show ads, or track you.

What the app stores

Your account. Your email address and a password (kept by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see the password). Firebase also gives your account an ID number.

What your family enters. Kids' first names (or nicknames), ages and colors; the points list, rewards and saving goals; the history of points added, taken away and cashed in; calendar activities with their titles, times, days and places; the family name; and settings such as points per dollar and the kid view lock question.

Who is in your family. The email address of each grown-up who joined, and the invite code.

On your device. A copy of your family's data, so the app opens quickly and works offline.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device advertising ID, or usage analytics. It has no ads and no third-party tracking. Kids don't create accounts or type in personal information; a grown-up decides what to enter about them.

How it's used and shared

Your family's data is used only to show it in the app and keep it in sync on the devices of the grown-ups in your family. Only people who have joined your family with its invite code can see it. We don't sell or rent it and don't share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which processes it for us to run the app. It is sent over encrypted connections.

Old iPad board (optional, off by default). If a grown-up turns it on in Settings, the kids' names, colors and point totals are published at a long, hard-to-guess web address so an older iPad can show them. Anyone who has that address can see those totals. Turning it off deletes that copy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account. If you're the last grown-up in your family, the family and everything in it are deleted too. If other grown-ups remain, your account is deleted and the family stays for them. See Delete your account for details, including how to ask us to do it for you.

You can also remove single kids, history entries or calendar items in the app at any time. Using the app without an account keeps everything on that device only; deleting the app removes it.

Children

Family Point Bank is meant to be set up and run by parents and other adults. It is not designed for children to create accounts. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
