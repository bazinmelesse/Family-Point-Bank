Help and contact

Family Point Bank: points, rewards and a family calendar for your kids.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email CONTACT_EMAIL. We usually reply within two days.

Getting started

Create an account with your email, tap Start a family, and add your kids. In Settings you'll find an invite code to share with the other parent: they install the app, create their own account, tap Join and enter the code. Points then show live on every grown-up's phone.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a link. Check your spam folder.

How do I put it on a wall iPad? Install the app on the iPad, sign in with either parent's account, and tap the kid view button. Leaving kid view asks the grown-up question you pick in Settings.

Is "cash out" real money? No. The app only keeps track of points and what they're worth at your family's rate. Any money is paid by you, outside the app.

Someone has our invite code who shouldn't. Settings → Make a new code. The old code stops working, and you can remove anyone from the list of grown-ups.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
